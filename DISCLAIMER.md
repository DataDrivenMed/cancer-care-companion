# Medical, Privacy, and Use Disclaimer

## Purpose
Cancer Care Companion is an organizational, educational, research, and patient-advocacy support tool. It can help users organize records, understand terminology, prepare questions, review evidence, and identify topics to discuss with the oncology team.

It is not a substitute for professional medical care.

## Medical boundaries
The tool does not:

- diagnose cancer or complications
- prescribe medications or dose changes
- select treatment for a patient
- provide guaranteed individualized prognosis
- determine clinical-trial eligibility
- replace instructions from the treating oncology team

For symptoms that may represent an emergency, users should follow their oncology team's emergency instructions and seek appropriate emergency evaluation. Research or AI interaction should never delay urgent care.

## Privacy
Privacy and regulatory obligations depend on the product, account type, organization, jurisdiction, contractual terms, and how data are handled.

Do not assume that every consumer AI service is appropriate for protected or confidential health information. Before entering identifiable medical information:

- review the service's current privacy and data-control settings
- follow the policies of the healthcare organization, employer, or institution involved
- use approved institutional tools when required
- minimize unnecessary identifiers

Direct identifiers such as names, medical record numbers, dates of birth, exact addresses, telephone numbers, and other identifying details must not be placed into public web searches or clinical-trial search queries generated by this project.

## Evidence limitations
Medical evidence changes over time. Trial status, regulatory approvals, professional recommendations, and site availability can change. Important findings should be verified against current authoritative sources and the treating team.

## User responsibility
Users remain responsible for deciding what information to share, confirming important medical facts with qualified healthcare professionals, and following applicable privacy and organizational policies.
